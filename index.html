Our story

Pre-loved fabric, new life

3Fluxe began at my sewing table in Limerick. I'm a self-taught sewer, and I believe good fabric deserves a second chance.

I buy clothes from charity shops across Ireland, take them apart, and stitch them into new pieces. Old jeans become bags. Worn dresses become caps and accessories. Your purchase keeps fabric out of landfill and supports the charities I buy from.

Stitching my story in fabric

  1. FoundPre-loved clothes chosen from Irish charity shops.
  2. Washed & unpickedEach garment is cleaned and taken apart by hand.
  3. Cut & stitchedThe fabric is cut into a new design and sewn piece by piece.
  4. YoursFinished, checked and posted from Limerick.
