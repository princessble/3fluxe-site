Our story

A second life for fabric.
A little space for me.

3Fluxe began at my sewing table in Limerick. I’m self-taught, and making is my way of slowing down and expressing what words sometimes can’t. For me, fashion is therapy: time to create, experiment, and feel like myself.

I buy clothes from charity shops across Ireland, take them apart, and stitch them into new pieces. Old jeans become bags. Worn dresses become caps and accessories. Giving these fabrics a second life is part of the joy. Buying from charity shops also supports the charities behind them.

Then I add Ankara, the bold wax-print fabric I grew up around. A panel, a trim, a lining. It's how I bring my African roots into everything I make here in Ireland.

Bold pattern, a connection to my African roots, and a personal touch in every piece.

  3. Cut & stitchedThe fabric is cut into a new design and sewn piece by piece.
  4. Finished with AnkaraA panel, trim or lining of African wax print makes it 3Fluxe.
