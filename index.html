Questions people ask

Where are you based?

Home salon in Limerick. The exact address is shared on WhatsApp when you book — it keeps things safe and personal.

Do you sell your shampoo and oil?

Right now, our handmade shampoo and growth oil are used during your treatments in the salon. Retail bottles are coming once our product registration is complete — members will hear first.

Do I need to bring my own hair?

Crown Care members bring their own extensions — your hands-on styling is what's included. Crown & Glow members receive a one-time welcome supply of scalp-friendly extensions, prepped and waiting — after that, you bring your own for each new style. Crowned Life members never bring hair — all your extensions are supplied, every cycle, from your Named Shelf. For pay-as-you-go braids, we can supply prepped extensions for a small fee, or you bring your own — just tell us when booking.

Why every two months?

Because that's your hair's natural rhythm. Protective styles are made to last 6–8 weeks — restyling every four weeks stresses your edges and steals your length. Every cycle you get a full session: takedown, care, and a fresh style of your choice. We protect hair here, we don't punish it.

Can someone use my membership if I skip a cycle?

Memberships are personal — your record, your bonnet, your prepped hair are built around you. But once a year you can gift your session to a sister, daughter or friend for a simple style — we call it the Sister Slot. Just arrange it on WhatsApp ahead of time. Sharing a chapter of your story is welcome here. 🩷

Is there a registration fee?

Yes — a one-time €30 registration for every tier, non-refundable. It's not just commitment: it includes your welcome satin bonnet and sets up your Crown Journey record before your first visit.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes — one month's notice on WhatsApp, no drama, no hard feelings.

What if I can't provide my own extensions?

No worries at all — if bringing your own extensions is a stress, we can supply scalp-friendly, prepped extensions at an affordable fee for Crown Care members and pay-as-you-go clients. Just tell us when booking and we'll have your hair ready and waiting. And every visit, whichever package you're on, comes with light refreshments — you're in Mama's house, after all. 🩷
